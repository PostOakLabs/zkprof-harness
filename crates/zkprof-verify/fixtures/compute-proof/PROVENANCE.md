# Test fixture provenance — compute-proof receipts

The `*.receipt.json` files here are **read-only copies** of real AINumbers
`compute-proof` receipts, fetched from the estate's public fixture directory,
the `chaingraph/kernels/fixtures/compute-proof/` directory of
https://github.com/PostOakLabs/ainumbers. The receipts are that repository's
content, and its LICENSE files govern them.

They were **not** generated by this repository. They are risc0 `groth16-bn254`
receipts (system: risc0, receiptFormat: groth16-bn254), each a 256-byte Groth16
BN254 seal over a claim binding an imageId to a journal digest. Their journal is
stored as decoded JSON; see `docs/JOURNAL-BYTES-HYPOTHESIS.md` for the
journal-byte-contract finding (JCS-canonical).

Copied 2026-09-01 (the four singled-out `<name>.receipt.json` fixtures) and
2026-09-02 (the full 312-receipt corpus sweep, `corpus-sweep-8`), read-only
(zero writes to the AINumbers repo). Provenance field in each:
`system: "risc0"`, `receiptFormat: "groth16-bn254"`. The receipt bytes are the
estate's, not ours; the only artifact this repo adds is the verifier that checks
them.

## The full corpus sweep (batch provenance note, 2026-09-02)

The files named `<estate-name>.receipt.json` (e.g.
`art-605-merkle-airdrop-proof-verifier.receipt.json`) are a **read-only copy of
the entire 312-receipt estate corpus** named verbatim by their estate basename.
They were copied in one batch (2026-09-02) to make the "four receipts agree"
statement into a corpus statement — `docs/TWIN-CORPUS-SWEEP.md` runs BOTH
verifiers (the risc0 crate and the JS twin) over every one. No estate file was
written; the copies are byte-identical to the estate originals. Many receipts
share the universal imageId `sha256:a1a0bc89…` (see `docs/VERIFIER-TWIN-AGREEMENT.md`),
so the single-image claim holds across the corpus; the receipts that use a
different imageId are where the corpus adds coverage.
